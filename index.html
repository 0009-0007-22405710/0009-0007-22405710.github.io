About Me

Dr. Palash Das is a medical graduate, public health professional, researcher, and health science educator from Bangladesh. His academic and professional interests focus on public health, epidemiology, disease surveillance, health education, and evidence-based healthcare.

He holds a Bachelor of Medicine and Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS) degree from Chattogram Medical College, Bangladesh, and a Master of Public Health (MPH) degree from Brunel University London, United Kingdom. He serves as an Instructor of Health Science at the University of the People.

His interests include epidemiological research, non-communicable diseases, diabetes prevention, disease monitoring, health promotion, and translating scientific evidence into practical public health strategies.
